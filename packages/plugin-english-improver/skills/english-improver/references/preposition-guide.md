# Preposition Guide for Technical English

## Most Common Preposition Mistakes

### Explain
**Wrong:** "Can you explain me this code?"
**Right:** "Can you explain this code to me?" or "Can you explain how this code works?"

### Interested
**Wrong:** "I'm interested about machine learning"
**Right:** "I'm interested in machine learning"

### Good/Bad
**Wrong:** "I'm good in Python"
**Right:** "I'm good at Python" or "I'm skilled in Python"

### Married/Related
**Wrong:** "She's married with a developer"
**Right:** "She's married to a developer"

### Depend
**Wrong:** "It depends of the situation"
**Right:** "It depends on the situation"

### Solution/Answer
**Wrong:** "The solution for this problem"
**Right:** "The solution to this problem"

### Expert
**Wrong:** "He's expert of JavaScript"
**Right:** "He's an expert in JavaScript"

### Work
**Wrong:** "I work about AI projects"
**Right:** "I work on AI projects" or "I work with AI"

### Ask
**Wrong:** "Can I ask you a question about?"
**Right:** "Can I ask you a question about this?"

### Afraid/Scared
**Wrong:** "I'm afraid of making mistakes"
**Right:** "I'm afraid of making mistakes" (this one is correct!)

## Technical Context Prepositions

### Database
**Work with databases**
**Connect to the database**
**Store in the database**
**Query from the database**

### APIs
**Call the API**
**Send data to the API**
**Get data from the API**
**Integrate with the API**

### Code
**Write code in Python**
**Compile the code**
**Run the code**
**Debug the code**

### Projects
**Work on a project**
**Lead a project**
**Contribute to a project**
**Deploy a project**

### Teams
**Work with a team**
**Manage a team**
**Join a team**
**Collaborate with teammates"

## Memory Tricks

### "In" for Areas of Study
- Interested **in** (subjects/fields)
- Major **in** (computer science)
- Specialize **in** (machine learning)
- Expert **in** (technology)

### "At" for Skills
- Good **at** (programming)
- Bad **at** (debugging)
- Skilled **at** (problem-solving)
- Terrible **at** (documentation)

### "On" for Work/Focus
- Work **on** (projects/tasks)
- Focus **on** (priorities)
- Concentrate **on** (problems)
- Depend **on** (factors)

### "To" for Direction/Answers
- Solution **to** (problems)
- Answer **to** (questions)
- Married **to** (people)
- Similar **to** (things)
